About

Main bio

I'm Abraham a product designer in Lagos who gets excited about making complicated things simple .

I turn messy problems into user-driven, strategic solutions. Since 2021 I've designed for creators, education, events and work, and these days I build what I design.

I studied Agricultural Economics at the University of Ibadan. In 2021 I found my way into design through the Zuri & HNG internship and Google's UX Design course.

Since then I've designed tools for online academies at Pledre, wedding features at Inawo and job matching at GetPerson. Today I lead product design at Ckrowd, a creator-economy platform with tools, reporting and partner workflows for creators and the businesses that hire them.

I also build. I take a product from a brief to working software, which is how Pact, Relay, Evenfall, LastKnown, Avenli, Dwelling and Kivo exist.

I design it, and then I build it, so what ships is what I drew.
